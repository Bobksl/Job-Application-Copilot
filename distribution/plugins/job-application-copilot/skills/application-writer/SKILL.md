# Application Writer

Write copy-ready proposals only. The pipeline owns approvals and optional Gmail-draft creation.

## Resume Redline

Work section by section. Put every proposed change in one consolidated table with:

- Change ID
- Location
- Before
- After
- JD requirement addressed
- Supporting fact IDs
- Reason for the change
- Estimated line impact

Ask for one approval and one feedback response covering the full table. After feedback, return the final table and replacement bullets in document order for manual paste-back.

Use achievement framing only when the fact bank supports the action, context, and result. Do not manufacture metrics. Bracketed questions are allowed in proposals but must remain outside final copy-ready text until answered.

Prefer concise bullets or an explicitly proposed low-value removal over font, margin, or spacing changes. Limit exact ATS phrases to those needed for accurate, natural wording.

## Cover Letter

Research current company needs when requested. Use the JD and official sources first, then reputable financial reporting. Default to the preceding 12 months; use an older mission statement only when it remains current. Label inference and do not claim a company is struggling without direct evidence.

Deliver in this order:

1. Three distinct opening concepts
2. One four-sentence contribution paragraph linking two or three verified achievements
3. One mission-alignment paragraph
4. Three closing options, each under three sentences
5. One combined copy-ready draft after the user chooses

Keep source citations in the analysis and case record. Do not force citations into the letter unless requested. Do not offer or perform direct template editing.

## Email

Draft recipient, subject, and body only when email is the actual application route. Keep it short, specific, and grounded in fact IDs. After approval, the pipeline may create an unsent draft; this skill never sends it.

## Style

Read [writing-rules.md](references/writing-rules.md). Use active verbs, direct sentences, and a confident professional voice. Remove robotic transitions, inflated adjectives, repetition, and generic praise.

Before handoff, ensure every candidate claim has fact IDs and every company claim has a recorded source or is labelled inference.
